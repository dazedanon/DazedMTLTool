# Welcome to DazedTL

DazedTL helps turn Japanese game text into English by sending it to an AI translation service.
It works best with **RPG Maker** and **WOLF RPG** games.

You do not need to understand programming to follow this Guide. Some later cleanup is easier with
an AI helper such as Cursor or GitHub Copilot, but the Guide will tell you when it is needed.

## Where should I begin?

1. Open **Before You Start** in the list on the left.
2. Follow **First Translation** one step at a time.
3. Use **RPG Maker Steps** or **WOLF RPG Steps** when you need more detail.
4. If you get lost, read **Full Example** to see the whole process from beginning to end.

## The basic idea

You will:

1. Tell DazedTL which game you want to translate.
2. Let it make working copies of the game's text.
3. Translate a small part first.
4. Put that translated text back into the game.
5. Play the game, find mistakes, and fix them.

> **Important:** Keep an untouched copy of the game before you begin. Never make your only copy
> the one you are editing.

When you are ready, click **Open Workflow** at the bottom of this page.
