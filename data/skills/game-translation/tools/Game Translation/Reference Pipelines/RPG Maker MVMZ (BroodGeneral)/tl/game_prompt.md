# Brood General Succubus (サキュバス将軍の兵力増産所) — game-specific guidance

## Setting & premise
"Brood General Succubus" (lit. *Succubus General's Troop Production Facility*) is a "Harassla" (孕み&スラッシュ, breed-and-slash) RPG by 丸宝堂/marupon (RPG Maker MZ, 2023). **Einaike** (アインアイク; *romanization TENTATIVE — verify against DLsite/title art; could parse Ein-Eik/Einaik*), the demon army's feared **Purple-Flame General** (紫焔将) and one of the **Three Generals** (三鼎将), suffers her first defeat in the centuries-long war against the **natives** (原住民) — the demons' othering, aboriginal-flavored slur for humankind; keep that contemptuous register, never neutralize to plain "humans." The natives alone can produce rare super-soldiers called **Heroes** (英雄). Stripped of rank, she is sentenced to **Cetus** (ケートゥス), an ancient, decay-frozen breeding facility where a confined female cannot leave until she conceives and gives birth. There she is repeatedly raped and impregnated by the facility's monsters, prisoners, and jailers to mass-produce ever-stronger demon soldiers (兵力増産) — a fate secretly ordained by the **Demon Lord** (Red-Crown King McDonnell) on the **Dark God**'s oracle, because her offspring grow stronger each generation. Keep two threads straight: who Einaike *was* outside (a respected general) versus what she *is* inside Cetus (an anonymous, used-up sow) — nearly every tormentor mocks that fall.

**Lore chain (don't conflate):** the **natives/原住民** are the rank-and-file humans who *birth* Heroes. The **Dark God** (邪神) is the cosmic entity behind/worshipped-as the origin of humankind whose oracle drives the plot — it is *not* the human foot-soldiers. Endgame turns cosmic-horror (Leviathan; **Creator Goddess Vendia**).

## Formatting & control codes
- **RPG Maker MZ control codes** appear as **⟦n⟧ placeholders** — preserve **exactly** in count, order, and position; never translate, renumber, or delete.
  - **Two kinds:** *dynamic-text* codes inject names/numbers/breeding values — land them in the **grammatically correct English slot**; *formatting-only* codes (color/icon/wait) **stay put**.
  - **Adjacency:** codes mid-word or mid-onomatopoeia bracket the text they're attached to — do **not** move a code across its neighbor; preserve left/right neighbors.
- **Name box** is handled separately — translate per Cast, but **romanization must match Cast/Terminology exactly** (Furfur, Cetus, etc.) so body and box never diverge; do not merge box text into line text.
- **Honorifics:** *kept/romanized* = -sama/-dono/-kun (register/mockery: 優等生ドノ → "honor-student-dono"). *Translated* = 閣下=Your Excellency, 卿=Lord, 陛下=Your Majesty (never "-kakka").
