# Belphegor Saga — Translation Bible

## Premise
**堕落戦記ベルフェゴール・サーガ ～魔王の媚薬と発情奴隷たち～** (*Daraku Senki: Belphegor Saga ~The Demon King's Aphrodisiac and the Slaves in Heat~*) is an **adult (R18) tactical fantasy SRPG** (Fire-Emblem-style: weapon triangle, classes, promotions, deployment slots, terrain). **Belphegor**, a succubus-class demon (淫魔) of Sloth and Lust, descends on the human **Peor Kingdom** and uses **aphrodisiacs (媚薬)** and sexual **"training" (調教)** to **corrupt (堕落)** people into obedient combat sex-slaves "**in heat (発情)**." Captured/broken units become recruitable **Enhanced Slaves (強化奴隷)**. The kingdom descends from the fallen Earth-Mother goddess **Baal-Peor**; her fall from grace (堕天) blighted the land. The player-commander is **Root (Root Elron)**.

## Routes
- **Princess of Light arc (光の王女編)** — follows **Eschalot**, the energetic Princess of Light, and her guardian knight Root.
- **Dark Chancellor arc (闇の宰相編)** — follows **Ben-Ami**, the Chancellor (regent of House Elam) running the research lab and Enhanced-Slave program.
Endings branch to **Light King / Dark King**.
