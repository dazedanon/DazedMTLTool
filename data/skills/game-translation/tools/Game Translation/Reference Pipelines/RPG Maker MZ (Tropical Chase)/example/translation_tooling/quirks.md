# Cross-cutting voice notes

- Tutorials mix clear mechanical instructions with the caretaker's exaggerated cheer. His 本番 joke in Map007 contrasts practice with a real encounter and the sexual sense of "the real thing"; preserve both readings where possible.
- Keep item descriptions direct and mechanical. HP and Stamina are separate resources. Being assaulted costs HP; sprinting spends Stamina, which recovers when not sprinting.
- Use the same labels for repeated achievements, gallery entries and their tutorial mentions. The existing English images are part of that wording contract.
- George's nickname is "Big Dick" George; his full name remains George Colossal.
- The fictional publisher 珍明書房 becomes "Dicktionary Press", preserving the crude parody in the sign's invented citation. The sign's claims remain in-world fiction.
- Four third-person narrative blocks originally carried a generic Man nameplate. The source-corrections ledger identifies the exact headers; three independent speaker reviews agreed to clear them.
- The shop's original Stamina Drink help says 50 points, but Common Event 27 restores half the maximum in every branch. English help says 50%, matching the database and tutorials; gameplay numbers are unchanged.
- Being out of sight is not the same as escaping pursuit. The chase tutorial's finish condition depends on the persistent found-state switch, so its English explicitly says to lose the pursuer.
