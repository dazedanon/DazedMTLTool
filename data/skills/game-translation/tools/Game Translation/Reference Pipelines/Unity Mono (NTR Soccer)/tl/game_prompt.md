# NTR Soccer (Hizure) — Translation Bible

## Premise
**NTR Soccer** is an adult (R18) Unity visual-novel / raising sim by Hizure. The adult protagonist
**Hiroshi (浩史, surname Kinoshita 木下)** coaches/supports a youth soccer team on the road to the
national tournament while living alongside several heroines (**Mei めい, Saki 咲希, Shiori 詩織,
Rin 凛, Yumiko 由美子**). Gameplay loops through a **World Map** day cycle (Mon–Sun, morning→night
time slots), stat training (gym, jogging), soccer minigames against rival players
(**Jiku, Shinji, Souma, Yuuto**), part-time events, and an NTR/harassment track
(the "Harass" events) where the heroines are targeted by other men (Suspicious Man, Shady Man, mobs).
S.Event-* conversations are the adult scenes; Mei also does live streams ("Mei Stream").

## Tone & content
- Slice-of-life dialogue is casual and natural; keep it light and colloquial.
- Adult scenes: render sexual content directly where the JP is direct; don't euphemize, don't add crudeness that isn't there.
- The official EN in this game keeps Japanese honorifics: **Kinoshita-san, Mei-chan, Saki-chan, Nii-san, Mister (おじさん)**. Match that style.
- Text in （） is inner monologue/narration — keep the parentheses.
- ♥ ♪ … ！？ punctuation flavor stays (ASCII equivalents fine: ! ? ...).
- System/UI lines (name-box "System") are terse and neutral.

## Mechanical rules
- Preserve markup EXACTLY: `[pic=N]` picture tags at line start, `<color=#FF4200>…</color>`,
  `<b>…</b>`, `<i>…</i>`, `<size=N>…</size>`, `{0}`-style placeholders, and `\n` line breaks.
- Never output kana/kanji in the English.
- UI labels: terse imperatives ("Save", "Back", "Buy"); stat abbreviations (DEX, STA, SP) stay as-is.
- Day-of-week single-kanji labels map to Mon/Tue/Wed/Thu/Fri/Sat/Sun.
- Do not add quotes, speaker names, or translator notes that are not in the source.
