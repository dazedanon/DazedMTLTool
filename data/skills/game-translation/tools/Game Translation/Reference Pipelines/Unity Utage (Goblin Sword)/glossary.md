## Translation Notes

- Canarie is the protagonist-heavy speaker in the dialogue extract. Keep her voice consistent across scenes.
- Bird-like names such as Egret, Swan, and Owl should remain name-like, not re-explained.
- Use "Goblin A/B/C" for lettered speaker variants and keep the letter.
- Translate titles naturally in narration or UI, but preserve them as consistent proper epithets when used as speaker names.
- For compact UI strings, prioritize space and clarity over literary wording.
