# musi_dream — translation bible

This is a Japanese-to-English translation project using local files. The text
store contains 509 manually translated units / 574 occurrences. The image census
later identified the title 虫ドリーム and subtitle 寝落ちYtuberとバズの夜. The patch's
English logo reads **BUG DREAM — A Sleeping Ytuber's Viral Night**; this is a
localization choice, not a claim of an official English title. `musi_dream` remains
the project, game-folder and save identifier. The readme credits 歩路地（ほろち） of
250歩の路地. Do not invent release dates or character ages.

The contemporary story follows an unsuccessful video creator. After an exhausting
24-hour kiwi-themed recording, she discovers she failed to record the performance
and falls asleep. A large, talking insect-like creature enters her room. The game
uses clickable scene interactions and choices, with outcomes affected by counters
and earlier actions. Her camera remains relevant to the ending: footage, audience
reaction, and a later change of platform connect the opening comedy to the finale.

The material combines adult insect fantasy, bodily horror, sexual violence, and
dark comedy about chasing online views. Preserve who acts, who experiences an
event, whether someone is awake or aware, and the source's distinctions between
fear, sensation, and consent. Do not turn a character's involuntary reaction into
agreement, or add erotic detail beyond the source. Preserve the source register
and meaning, including uncomfortable material, without editorial commentary.

The principal voice contrast is an expressive creator speaking casually or to a
camera, the creature's rough and drawn-out colloquial speech, relatively direct
third-person narration, and a casual viewer's ending reactions. The glossary owns
the individual spellings, gender evidence, and voice cues. Trust the scene's
explicit referents over a name, a sprite filename, or a stereotyped speech style.
An empty `#` switches to narration. An engine sprite key alone is weak identity
evidence: `sanran_ch` can anchor a bubble in a composite image, and the surrounding
line must still determine who speaks.

Read the numbered scenario files in order. `scene2` through `scene6` contain loops
and branches, so adjacent source lines are not always consecutive spoken lines.
`scene7` contains alternative ending passages; `scene8` and `scene9` continue
specific outcomes. Preserve parallel choice wording and the direction of each
action. Consult the label and expression context rather than inventing a single
canonical route. Never merge identical dialogue across branches automatically.

Translation frame: contemporary domestic setting with fantastical intrusion;
present-day video-sharing culture; natural contemporary English; conservative
editorial name spelling where no official Latin spelling exists. No particular
mythological tradition has been established.

The user-requested image track follows `images/PROMPT.md` and
`images/image_glossary.json` alongside the main glossary. It adds 30 archive-image
replacements, comprising 28 manual redraws and two exact duplicates, from a visual
census of 517 entries / 448 unique images. Keep tutorial actions **Insert** and
**Stop** consistent with their baked screenshots, and indicators **DANGER** and
**WIMP** consistent across standalone and flattened copies. Use **Favorites** for
the browser menu and retain Ytube, Ytuber and Hdouga as fictional service names.
The shirt already reads **Kiwi** in Latin lettering and stays unchanged.

The text and image release is installed. Its fresh normal-route playtest covered
all nine scenes and returned to the title, with additional checks of the image
controls and captions. This does not mean every alternate route was individually
played; consult `reports/RELEASE_VALIDATION.json` for the exact tested scope.

The browser's recommendations are separate scene content; a thumbnail title does
not redefine the heroine's identity or age. One Hdouga listing image contains
sexual depictions explicitly labeled as minors and is excluded from editing and
the payload. Record its unchanged status as a coverage limitation. The separate
final-video image shows the main adult heroine and has its own translated caption.

All source transcription, translation and image lettering are manual and offline.
