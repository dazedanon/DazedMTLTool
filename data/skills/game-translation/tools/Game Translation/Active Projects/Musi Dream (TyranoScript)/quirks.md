# Cross-cutting voice rules

- Keep the source's fictional platform spellings Ytube, Ytuber, and Hdouga.
- Preserve the recording/view-count running joke, anchored by 録画, 再生数, and
  撮れてる. Keep the distinction between failing to record and starting a recording.
- Use natural contractions in casual speech. Narration should be readable prose,
  without adopting the speaker's slang by default.
- Preserve an honorific if one is actually present and meaningful; do not add any.
- Render expressive sounds in natural English with comparable intensity and
  pacing. Hearts, pauses, and laughter are tone cues, not permission to add actions.
- Choices are actionable phrases; nameplates are short labels; diagnostic messages
  explain what happened and what the player can do. Keep each field's function.
