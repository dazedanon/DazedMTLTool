# Dress Quest - エリスと七つのドレス

RPG Maker VX Ace, 18+, circle ぽいずん (Poison), version 1.13.
A corruption RPG: the heroine loses her armour, her rank and her dignity piece
by piece across five chapters, and the player watches a proud knight-commander
be worn down.

## Premise

**Eris** is a knight-commander of the Kingdom of **Baron**, titled 白蓮の騎士,
the Knight of the White Lotus. The game opens on her own soldiers sealing a
village whose people are turning into monsters: her standing order is that
nobody leaves, and she enforces it while trying to hold her men to a knight's
code. That contradiction - a decent woman carrying out an indecent order, and
paying for it - is the spine of the story.

From there she loses the Anfunk Armour that marks her rank and has to travel in
whatever else she can get: a china dress, a maid's dress, a shrine maiden's
robe, a mage's dress, a nun's habit, a tentacle "feeler" dress. Seven dresses,
each with its own stats, its own portrait set and its own way of being
humiliating in public. The whole game is built on that joke and takes it
seriously.

## Register - the thing that matters most

**おろち様 is "Great Orochi", and has NO GENDER.** Zipang's water god is
never seen and never described: the villagers say plainly that no one has ever
been shown its form, and the entire arc is goblins impersonating it. The
Japanese uses 彼/彼女 for it exactly zero times in 233 units. Do not write he,
him, his, she or her for Orochi - rephrase ("that god's wrath", "we'd never be
shown that form", "no demand ever came unbidden"). Guessing a gender both
invents information and deflates the reveal. "Great" rather than "Lord"/"Lady"
because it is the only localized honorific in English that stays neutral; -sama
is not kept, per the エリス様 rule below.

Eris has **two voices and the gap between them is the game**.

* **In command**: crisp, formal, used to being obeyed. She gives orders in the
  plain imperative, refers to her men as 騎士団, and her subordinates call her
  エリス様 - render that as **"Lady Eris"** when addressed, "Lady Eris" or
  "the commander" in reference. Her own first person is 私; she never boasts.
* **Cornered, stripped or being used**: the formality cracks but does not
  vanish. She keeps trying to speak properly while what is happening to her
  makes it impossible. Do not let her collapse into generic porn-speak - the
  contrast IS the content. Broken formality reads as "P-please, s-stop-", never
  as "fuck me harder" unless the source says exactly that.
* Her inner monologue is in （ ）. Keep it in ( ) and keep it interior: it is
  where she says what she will not say aloud.

Everyone else is placed against her:

* **Soldiers of Baron** are coarse and cruel to civilians, deferential to her
  face. Keep the two registers in one speaker.
* **Villagers and townspeople** are plain, rural, unpolished.
* **Nobles** are oily and formal, and enjoy it.
* **Monsters** mostly do not speak in sentences: guttural, short, present
  tense. Where one does speak, it is leering rather than eloquent.

## Places, and how to render them

The Kingdom of **Baron** (バロン): the capital 王都バロン is the Royal Capital
of Baron. Towns and regions are transliterated, not translated, unless the name
is a plain word:

    コロン村          Colon Village
    王都バロン        the Royal Capital of Baron / Baron Castle for バロン城
    マッスルーム      a fighting-arena town - the name is a pun on "muscle"
    レーゲンヘーレ / エミリオン / デュッセルドルフ   transliterate
    ジパング          Zipang - a deliberately Japan-flavoured region; its
                      speakers use samurai-drama diction (足軽 = foot soldier,
                      お菊 is a Japanese name, not a transliteration puzzle)

## The dresses

They are equipment AND plot objects, so the name must be identical in a
message, in the inventory and in the equip menu:

    アンファンクアーマー    Anfunk Armour (her knight's armour; ...Ｐ is the
                            upgraded version)
    チャイナドレス          China Dress
    メイドドレス            Maid Dress
    巫女ドレス              Shrine Maiden Dress
    マジックドレス          Magic Dress
    シスタードレス          Sister Dress
    フィーラードレス        Feeler Dress (the tentacle one)

## Content

This is an R18 game and the sexual content is the product, not an accident.
Translate it directly: explicit, physical, in the register the source uses.
Most of it is non-consensual or coerced, and it is written that way on purpose -
do not soften it, do not add distance, do not editorialise. Equally, do not
make it more crude than the Japanese: this author writes a lot of the worst
scenes in plain, almost clinical narration, and that flatness is the effect.

Onomatopoeia and moans become English sounds, never romaji.
